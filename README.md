# Spot Spatter Simulator

Interactive cross-section of a resistance spot weld that shows **why spatter happens** and how to prevent it.

**Live page:** https://jesusgod24.github.io/spot-spatter-sim/

## What you can do

- Pick a cause (tip alignment, gun angle, gap between sheets, spot near edge, tip shape, dirty surface, partial overlap, low force, short squeeze time, too much current/time, Ø16 → Ø13 tips) and press **NG** or **OK**.
- Watch the whole cycle: gun closes, force builds, current flows, the nugget grows, and spatter appears when the nugget outgrows the clamped zone.
- Drag a slider across the red OK/NG limit line. Limits can be saved per plant (stored only in your browser).
- Switch between 2- and 3-sheet stacks, Thai / English, and a presentation mode that shows OK and NG side by side.

## Note

This is a simplified teaching model. Figures are examples to show trends; they are not a weld schedule or a standard. Always follow the real standard and weld parameters for each spot.
